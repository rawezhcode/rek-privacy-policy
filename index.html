Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application REK (“the App”), package name com.rawezhcode.finance_manager, handles information. The App is published by rawezhcode (“we”, “us”).

REK is a finance and project manager for freelancers and companies. You use it to keep clients, projects, payments, and project updates.

In short: the records you enter stay on your device. We do not run an account server for the App, we do not sell your data, and we do not show ads. A Google Drive backup is optional and is stored in your own Google account.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves what you type in a local database on your phone or tablet. That can include:

  • Client details: name, contact, type (freelance or company), email, phone, address, and notes
  • Projects: title, description, budget, currency, status, category, and dates
  • Payments: amount, date, payment method, and notes
  • Project updates and related notes

This information is stored in the App’s private storage. We do not receive a copy of it, and it is not uploaded to a server that we operate.

2. Optional Google sign-in and Drive backup

Backup and restore are optional. They run only after you choose to sign in with Google from the Backup screen.

When you sign in, the App requests:

  • Your Google account email, so the App can show which account is signed in
  • Permission to create, read, and delete only the backup files that the App itself creates in Google Drive (drive.file). The App does not ask to browse the rest of your Drive

A backup is a copy of the local database file. It is uploaded to your Google Drive with a name that starts with finance_manager_backup_. You can list, restore, or delete those files from the App. Google processes that file under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not keep your Drive files on our own servers.

3. Firebase

The App initializes Firebase when it starts. It does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Authentication, or a cloud database for your records. Your clients, projects, and payments are not sent to Firebase. Google’s Firebase libraries may still exchange basic technical information needed to initialize the SDK, as described in Firebase privacy documentation.

4. Internet access

The App requests internet access so it can sign you in to Google, upload, list, download, or delete a Drive backup, and initialize Firebase. Day-to-day records stay on the device and do not need a network connection.

5. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • We do not use advertising identifiers and we do not show ads
  • We do not sell personal information or share it with data brokers
  • We do not create a REK user account on our servers

6. Third parties

The only third-party services the App contacts are Google services you choose to use for sign-in and Drive backup, and Firebase for app initialization:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase and Google Privacy

7. How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • Local records stay on the device until you delete them in the App or you uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the App’s local database from that device.
  • Google Drive backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them in the App or in Google Drive. Uninstalling the App does not delete those files.
  • Because we do not keep your records on our servers, there is no REK account for us to erase. If you have a question about your information, email us at the address below.

8. Security

Records are kept in the App’s private storage on your device. Transfers to Google Drive use Google’s authenticated APIs over HTTPS. You are responsible for the security of your device, your screen lock, and your Google account.

9. Children

REK is not directed to children under 13, or under the age required by the law of your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. The current version is always available at this URL. Please review it from time to time.

11. Contact
